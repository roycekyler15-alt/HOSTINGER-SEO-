DM Play Agent

Invite & Earn — Daily Bonus & Team Salary

The DM Play Agent Program rewards you for growing the community through a powerful referral and team-building system. Every registered user gets a unique referral code and link. When someone registers using your link and plays Dmplay Games, you earn commission automatically.

  • Free to join — just register on dmplaygame.net
  • Unique referral link for every user
  • Instant commission tracking inside the app
  • Daily bonuses for growing your team
  • Team salary based on total team deposit activity

How to Start Earning

  1. Login to the DM Play App with your registered mobile number and password.
  2. Go to the "Invite & Earn" section in your dashboard.
  3. Copy your unique referral link.
  4. Share on WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook or Instagram.
  5. Earn commission every time a referred user deposits and plays.

Commission is credited automatically to your Dmplay wallet. Track all referrals and earnings from the Agent Dashboard inside the app.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I become a DM Play Agent?

Register on dmplaygame.net, log in to the app, and go to the "Invite & Earn" section. Your unique referral link is generated automatically.

Is there a joining fee?

No. The DM Play Agent Program is completely free to join.

You earn commission when someone registers using your referral link and deposits money to play Dmplay Games.
