Aviator Game

Multiplier Cash-Out — Rules & Guide

Aviator is a crash-style multiplier game available on Dmplay Game. In each round, a multiplier increases from 1x upward while an on-screen plane flies. Users must cash out before the plane flies away — if it does, the stake for that round is lost.

Round mechanics, minimum and maximum stakes, and auto cash-out features may vary, so always check the current rules displayed on the platform before participating.

How to Play Aviator

  1. Complete Your Login — open Dmplay and log in to access your dashboard.
  2. Open Aviator — select "Aviator" from the games section.
  3. Place Your Bet — enter a stake within the platform limits before the round begins.
  4. Watch the Multiplier — once the round starts, the multiplier increases from 1x upward.
  5. Cash Out in Time — tap the cash-out button before the plane flies away. Your payout = stake × multiplier at cash-out.
  6. If the Plane Flies Away First — the round ends, and any un-cashed stake is lost for that round.
  7. Optional Auto Cash-Out — set a target multiplier and let the system cash out automatically when it is reached.
  8. Check Your Wallet — successful cash-outs are credited to your wallet.

How the Multiplier Works

The multiplier starts at 1.00x and increases during the round. Higher multipliers mean bigger potential payouts — but also a higher risk that the plane flies away before you cash out.

Aviator Features

  • Live Multiplier — watch the multiplier rise in real time
  • Manual Cash-Out — tap to cash out at any moment
  • Auto Cash-Out — set a target multiplier for automatic cash-out
  • Round History — view previous multipliers for reference only
  • Live Player Feed — see other users' cash-outs in the round (where available)

Important Aviator Tips

  • Always check the current game rules before participating.
  • Decide your cash-out target before the round begins.
  • Don't assume previous multipliers predict the next round.
  • Set a personal spending limit before you start.
  • Never chase losses.
  • Only participate if you are legally permitted to do so in your location.
Note: Aviator outcomes are random, and previous rounds do not guarantee future results.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Aviator?

Aviator is a crash-style multiplier game where users must cash out before the plane flies away.

How long does an Aviator round last?

Rounds are usually short — typically under a minute — but duration depends on how high the multiplier climbs before the crash.

Can I predict when the plane will fly away?

No. Aviator outcomes are random, and there is no reliable way to predict when a round will end. Treat it as entertainment, not guaranteed income.

What is auto cash-out?

Auto cash-out lets you set a target multiplier. When the round reaches that multiplier, the system cashes out automatically — even if you are not watching.

Where can I play Aviator?

On the Dmplay website or inside the Dmplay App. Complete your DM Play Login first.
